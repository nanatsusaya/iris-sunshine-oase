photos in conversation:

- the **Moments flyer**, three panels;
- the **winter opening-hours card**;
- the **summer opening-hours card**.

This file is the verbatim transcription of them, and the documented source that the confirmed entries
in `src/content/` name. The photos are **not** in the repository. They show nothing a reader needs
beyond this text, and they are not the studio's to publish as images in their own right.

Like `docs/content/`, the quoted material stays **German and unmodified**, and the framing is English.
Where print and handwriting differ, both are recorded, and the owner's answer settles which one applies.
